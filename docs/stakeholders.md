# Stakeholders

## Billing Employee
Creates invoices, enters required billing information, and submits invoices for review.

### Needs
- Simple invoice creation
- Clear required fields
- Ability to submit invoices
- Visibility into invoice status
- Feedback when corrections are needed

## Reviewer / Manager
Reviews submitted invoices for accuracy and completeness before final approval.

### Needs
- Access to submitted invoice details
- Ability to identify errors
- Ability to approve for the next step or reject for correction
- Visibility into invoice history and status

## Approver
Makes the final approval or rejection decision on reviewed invoices.

### Needs
- Clear invoice details
- Review history
- Ability to approve or reject
- Recorded approval or rejection decision
- Audit trail of the final outcome
