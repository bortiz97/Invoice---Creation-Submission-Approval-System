# Business Problem and Scope

## Business Problem

The invoice creation, review, and approval process can involve multiple employees and several steps before an invoice is finalized. When invoice information, corrections, approvals, and status updates are not managed through one centralized workflow, it can be difficult to determine where an invoice is in the process and whether additional action is required.

This can result in delayed invoicing, missed corrections, duplicate work, and difficulty tracking responsibility for invoice decisions.

The Invoice Creation, Submission & Approval System will provide a centralized workflow for creating invoices, reviewing invoice information, correcting errors, and recording approval or rejection decisions.

## Project Scope

### Must Have
- Create an invoice
- Enter client and invoice information
- Add service or charge details
- Calculate the invoice total
- Submit an invoice for review
- Review invoice information
- Approve or reject an invoice
- Track invoice status
- Record rejection reasons
- Allow rejected invoices to be corrected and resubmitted

### Should Have
- Display invoice status history
- Record reviewer and approver actions
- Record timestamps for important status changes

### Could Have
- Search and filter invoices
- Dashboard showing invoices by status
- Additional reporting features

### Out of Scope
The project will not:
- Send actual invoices to clients
- Process real payments
- Integrate with a real accounting system
- Store real client or company financial data
